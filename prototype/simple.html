Questions, answered straight

Is my ZEC ever sold?

No. It sits as collateral on Rhea Finance (NEAR). A bounded loan — your 30–50% risk setting — is taken against it in USDC, and that borrowed money does the earning in the curated Aerodrome pool you chose on Base. Borrowing against an asset — rather than selling it — is also why entering doesn't dispose of your ZEC (mechanics, not tax advice).

There's no login. How do I get back to my position?

Your locked Zcash address is your identity. Every position is keyed on-chain by the address you set at deposit — the only address funds can ever exit to. Close the tab, restart the computer, open the app on another device: type that address into Find your position and it comes back. Looking up is read-only and safe to do from anywhere: seeing a position never lets anyone move it, because there is nowhere else for the money to go. This browser also keeps a local copy for convenience; "forget this device" removes only that copy.

In the live product, actions that change a position (withdraw, claim, change settings) additionally ask you to prove the address is yours — a one-time passkey created at deposit, with a fallback of sending a tiny amount from that same address. That stops a stranger who merely knows your address from withdrawing to you at a bad time.

Who holds my ZEC while it moves?

For about two minutes on the way in and out, your ZEC rides NEAR Intents — the same rails built into the Zashi and Zodl wallets, which move over $1B a month. During that window the bridge's operator controls the transit address; if anything fails, funds auto-refund to your address. We tell you this because you should know exactly who holds what, when — it's in every deposit screen, not buried here.

What happens if ZEC crashes?

The loan is your chosen 30–50% of your collateral's value — at the default Sheltered setting, ZEC has to fall 57% before liquidation is even possible (43% at Steady, 29% at Working hard). Before that ever gets close, protection kicks in automatically, in order: your earnings repay the loan first; if that's not enough, we unwind just enough of the working position to restore safety; in an extreme crash, everything unwinds and your ZEC sits safe as plain collateral. You'll see each step in Activity with its transaction id.

Where does the yield come from — and why is today's number so low?

Only from AERO emissions. Your pool position is staked in its Aerodrome gauge; staked positions earn the gauge's AERO emissions instead of trading fees (those go to veAERO voters). Against that, the position loses value to impermanent loss every time the price leaves its range and the engine re-centers it, and the borrowed USDC costs interest. We model all three from on-chain data and only offer a pool when the result is positive. At the rates sampled 2026-08-31, no curated pool clears the borrow cost — so the honest number is near zero, and the app says so rather than showing you the pool's headline rate.

What if the engine can't deposit the whole amount?

The pool engine takes your USDC, swaps roughly half into the other token itself, and mints the position in one transaction. Whatever doesn't fit the exact ratio — usually under 1% — it refunds in the same transaction. Oilskin keeps that refund inside your position as idle balance and puts it back to work at the next compound; it is never sent anywhere except your position or your locked address.

What are the fees?

15% of earnings to the pool engine that manages liquidity, then 10% of the remainder to Oilskin — 23.5% all-in, only ever on earnings. Nothing on deposits, nothing on withdrawals, nothing on your principal, ever. Every yield number you see is already net of all fees.

Can I get my ZEC back whenever I want?

Yes — withdrawal can never be paused; it's written into the contract, not a policy. Withdrawals unwind your share (repaying the loan's part automatically) and arrive as native ZEC at your locked address, usually within a few minutes.

What about my privacy?

Fund your deposit from your shielded balance — that's the default guidance, and it keeps your history private. The strategy itself lives on public chains (NEAR + Base), and we say so plainly. Payouts land at your transparent address and your wallet shields them (Zodl does it automatically). We generate a fresh deposit address every time so deposits are never linked to each other.

Can Oilskin freeze or redirect my funds?

No. Withdrawal is exempt from every pause. Funds can only exit to the address you locked at deposit. The operator can manage yield — it can never touch principal or change your address.

How Oilskin works

The whole system in plain language. Every claim on this page is checkable — every action in Activity carries its transaction id, and the code is public.

The strategy, end to end

  • 1 · Deposit. Send ZEC from any Zcash wallet to a fresh deposit address (QR / ZIP-321). Fund it from your shielded balance — that's the default guidance.
  • 2 · Collateral. Your ZEC is supplied as collateral on Rhea Finance (NEAR). It is never sold.
  • 3 · Borrow. USDC is borrowed against it at your risk setting — 30–50% of collateral value.
  • 4 · Earn. That USDC works in the single curated pool you picked, staked in the pool's Aerodrome gauge and rebalanced automatically. Earnings arrive as AERO emissions and compound whenever they cover costs 3×.
  • 5 · Home. Withdrawals unwind your share, repay the loan's part automatically, and arrive as native ZEC at your locked address.

Your risk setting — what it actually sets

One dial, three positions. It sets how much is borrowed against your ZEC (your distance to the liquidation line, threshold 70%) and how the pool position is run — the engine preset, its range width, and how long it waits out of range before re-centering:

SettingBorrowedZEC must fallRange widthRebalance delayEngine preset
Sheltered (default)30%57%±25% (±12.5% correlated pairs)48 h out of rangeConservative
Steady40%43%±8% (±4%)12 hModerate
Working hard50%29%±1.5% (±0.75%)2 hAggressive

A tighter range earns more emissions per dollar while the price sits inside it — and leaves it far more often, losing more to impermanent loss at every re-centering. Wider ranges rebalance a few times a year; the tightest, hundreds. Each pool card shows exactly these numbers for the setting you have selected. Your setting locks at deposit; to change it, withdraw and redeposit.

Where the number comes from

Every pool position is staked in its Aerodrome gauge. Staked positions earn the gauge's AERO emissions in lieu of trading fees — the fees go to veAERO voters — so all yield is emissions, and we never quote a fee rate. Three things are modeled per pool and per setting, from on-chain data:

  • Emissions at your width. Read from the gauge (rewardRate) and the pool's staked in-range liquidity, then scaled to a position of your range width — a wide position holds less liquidity per dollar than the tight positions it competes with, so it earns a smaller share. Sampled 2026-08-31 (AERO $0.48).
  • Impermanent loss & rebalancing. Simulated over a year at each pair's realized volatility (ETH 55%, BTC 40%, ETH/BTC 33%; Gecko daily candles Mar–Aug 2026), re-centering after the setting's delay, with the swap cost of every rebalance.
  • Costs. 15% engine fee then 10% Oilskin fee on emissions; the live USDC borrow rate on the borrowed slice.

A pool is offered at a setting only if net emissions after IL beat the borrow cost. Sampled 2026-08-31, with USDC borrowing at 13.47%:

Straight talk: at these emissions and this borrow rate, no curated pool clears the bar at any setting. Wider settings lose less but earn less; the borrowed dollar costs 13.47% and nothing here pays that after impermanent loss. Aerodrome emissions and Rhea's borrow rate both move week to week — the app recomputes from live data and offers a pool the moment it qualifies. Until then, the honest yield is the ZEC supply rate on Rhea (0.04%), and the app will not open a leveraged position for you.

This is a model, built to be checkable: the sampling script, the simulation and its assumptions ship in the repo (services/yield/scripts/lp-sim.py, services/yield/samples/). Once the yield service has backfilled the engine's real closed-position history, realized ranges replace the model on every card — history beats simulation.

Live mode & the realized-return band

This page ships with a dated sample baked in. Run the Oilskin yield service beside it (double-click start-demo.command in the repo) and the numbers go live: current gauge emissions, the live Rhea borrow and ZEC supply rates, and — once the service has backfilled the engine's on-chain history — a realized-return band per pool instead of a model. The band is the middle half (p25–p75, weighted by position size) of what real, closed positions in that pool actually kept over the trailing 30 days — impermanent loss, rebalances, and all fees included. It is history, not a model, and never a promise. Until the backfill has run, cards say "pending backfill" rather than inventing a range.

The protection ladder

  • 1 · Earnings repay first. If ZEC falls, your earnings pay the loan down before anything else moves.
  • 2 · Partial unwind. Not enough? Just enough of the working position unwinds to restore a safe distance.
  • 3 · Full unwind. In a severe crash, everything unwinds and your ZEC sits as plain collateral until you say otherwise.

Every step is automatic, around the clock, and logged in Activity with its transaction id.

Finding your position again

There is no account and no password. Your locked Zcash address keys the position on-chain, so any device can look it up read-only — that is safe because funds can only ever exit to that same address. This browser also keeps a local copy (nothing sensitive — the same public facts the chain holds). In the live product, actions that change a position (withdraw, claim, adjust) require proof that the address is yours: a passkey bound at deposit — synced by your OS across your devices — with a fallback of a tiny proof transaction from the locked address. Without it, a stranger who learned your address could still only ever withdraw to you, but could do it at a moment you didn't choose.

Fees

15% of earnings to the pool engine at harvest, then 10% of what remains to Oilskin — 23.5% total, only ever on earnings. Nothing on deposits, withdrawals, or principal — ever. Example: your position earns $100 of emissions → the engine takes $15, Oilskin takes $8.50, and $76.50 stays in your position. Every yield number shown is already net of all fees.

Refunds and partial deposits

The engine deposits single-sided: it takes your USDC, swaps part of it into the pool's other token, and mints the position in one transaction. Whatever cannot fit the exact ratio at that price — typically well under 1% — is refunded in the same transaction. Oilskin books that refund to your position as idle balance and redeploys it at the next compound; on withdrawal it is paid out with everything else. A failed bridge transfer refunds to your locked address automatically.

What can actually go wrong

  • A crash beyond your buffer. If ZEC falls past your setting's line faster than the ladder can act, the loan's share of collateral can be liquidated. This is the core risk — it's why the buffers are wide and the ladder exists.
  • Impermanent loss. The working position loses value relative to simply holding whenever the price moves and the engine re-centers. It is modeled, shown, and the reason most pools are not offered today.
  • Venue risk. Rhea's margin sub-system — which Oilskin does not use — was exploited in April 2026; lending resumed in May and has run consistently since. You should hear that from us, not find it later: exposure is capped per venue and monitored independently.
  • Bridge transit. For ~2 minutes each way, funds ride NEAR Intents under its operator's control. Failed transfers auto-refund; that window is the exposure.
  • Smart-contract risk. Code can have bugs. Independent audit before mainnet, and withdrawal can never be paused — a contract invariant, not a policy.

Privacy

Fund deposits from your shielded balance and your history stays private — deposit addresses are fresh every time and never linked to each other. The strategy legs (NEAR, Base) are public chains and we say so plainly. Payouts arrive at your transparent address; your wallet shields them on arrival (Zodl does this automatically).

Deposit size

No Oilskin cap. One position per address (new deposits top it up), floor 0.25 ZEC — below that, bridge fees eat the deposit. Very large deposits are bounded only by constraints outside our control: chiefly the USDC actually available to borrow on Rhea at that moment, and the pool's own depth. Once live, the app shows that headroom before you send anything.
