Have ZEC on Coinbase? How it becomes cbZEC on Base — and what to know first

cbZEC is Coinbase Wrapped ZEC, 1:1 backed by ZEC in Coinbase custody. There is exactly one way to get newly-minted cbZEC, and it is Coinbase's:

  1. Deposit ZEC to your Coinbase account. Coinbase ZEC balances are transparent addresses; a send from any Zcash balance lands there in the open.
  2. Choose "Send ZEC on Base" and give your Base wallet address. cbZEC arrives at that address.
  3. Connect that wallet here. In v1 cbZEC can be swapped or LP'd; as collateral it is v1.1 (no lending market on Base yet).
Before you start — three facts, stated plainly.
  • Jurisdiction. Wrap/unwrap is unavailable in 100+ jurisdictions — all of the EEA, Australia, Brazil, Canada, Japan, Singapore, and New York. Effectively the US ex-NY (UK unverified). Non-US users can still buy cbZEC on Base and use every other feature; cbBTC/WETH users are unaffected.
  • KYC and custody. Full identity verification both ways. Coinbase knows the person, the amount and the destination address. This entry is custodial and identity-linked; this product makes no privacy claim about it.
  • Transparent-only exit. Unwrapping pays ZEC to transparent addresses only. Fees, minimums and confirmation counts are unpublished.
Counterfeit warning. A cluster of fake "ZEC" tokens share the 0xb2000… address prefix. The only cbZEC is (8 decimals, name "Coinbase Wrapped ZEC"). Verify the full address, not the prefix — the app pins it and refuses look-alikes.
Issuer powers you should know about: cbZEC is a B20 token, not a plain ERC-20. Its issuer can block, pause, seize (burn blocked balances) and rebase balances via a multiplier. cbBTC has none of these powers. Whether restrictive policies are configured on cbZEC is an on-chain read we do before integrating — and every contract we write assumes its balance can move.

Questions, answered straight

Is my collateral ever sold?

No. It sits as collateral on Aave v3 on Base. A bounded loan — your setting — is taken against it in USDC, and that borrowed money does the earning in the curated Aerodrome pool you chose. Borrowing against an asset rather than selling it is also why entering doesn't dispose of it (mechanics, not tax advice).

Who holds my funds?

You do. Your wallet owns an Oilskin account — a small per-user smart account with no admin, no upgrade and no fee logic. The Aave position and the pool position live under that account. Oilskin's router is stateless: one transaction does supply → borrow → deploy, and it asserts that its balance of every token it touched is unchanged when the transaction ends. A keeper may act only through a permission you grant — one target, one function, per-token daily limits, an expiry date, revocable. What that does not mean is "no operator": the next answer says plainly what Oilskin can still do.

      Can I get my collateral back whenever I want?

      Yes. Withdrawal unwinds your pool share, repays the loan's part automatically, and returns collateral to your wallet — in one transaction from your own account. There is no pause on exits because there is nothing to pause: you can always execute the unwind yourself from your account.

      Where does the ZEC come in?

      cbZEC — Coinbase Wrapped ZEC on Base. The entry is Coinbase's: KYC'd, custodial, US-ex-NY, transparent addresses only. It is identity-linked, and this product makes no privacy claim about it. In v1 cbZEC can be swapped or LP'd; as collateral it needs a lending market that does not exist yet (v1.1). cbBTC and WETH work today. See the explainer on the Earn tab.

      What is emissions-only?

      Staked Aerodrome positions earn AERO incentives in lieu of trading fees (fees go to veAERO voters). Every yield number here assumes emissions only, after the engine's and Oilskin's fees, minus impermanent-loss drag, minus the live borrow rate. A pool whose gauge has no emissions — the cbZEC/USDC pool today — earns nothing and is never offered.

      How Oilskin works

      The whole system in plain language. Every derived number on this page is computed from the verified Aave parameters below — none is typed.

      The strategy, end to end

      • 1 · Connect. Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, WalletConnect or any EIP-6963 wallet on Base. Your first deposit creates your Oilskin account (CREATE2 — its address is known before it exists).
      • 2 · Collateral. cbBTC or WETH is pulled via Permit2 and supplied to Aave v3 on Base under your account. It earns Aave's supply rate and is never sold.
      • 3 · Borrow. USDC is borrowed against it at your setting, at Aave's live variable rate.
      • 4 · Earn. The USDC is deployed by the stateless router into one curated Aerodrome pool via the Snuggle engine, staked in the pool's gauge. It earns AERO emissions — in lieu of trading fees — and is rebalanced by the engine.
      • 5 · Home. Withdrawals unwind your share, repay the loan's part, and return collateral to your wallet. All from your account, in one transaction.

      The pool menu — emissions-only, gated

      Your borrowed USDC works in one pool at a time. A pool is offered at a setting only when emissions × (1 − fees) − IL drag > the live Aave USDC borrow rate. Emissions are the gauge's AERO rate at sampling; IL drag is the model's impermanent-loss cost at the pool's width (σ² ÷ 4·f(w)). Nothing is curated by hand.

      Two models, and the band between them

      The published slice comes from a closed form that assumes the position never leaves its price band. A real concentrated position spends part of the year outside it earning nothing, and pays a swap cost every time it is re-centred. So the gate prices every cell a second time with a Monte-Carlo-calibrated form and offers only when both clear the borrow rate. Where they disagree the cell sits inside the model's own uncertainty and is refused — and that band is precisely the one that would otherwise have become an offer.
