    The risk list

      Have ZEC on Coinbase? Three steps to cbZEC on Base

      cbZEC is Coinbase Wrapped ZEC, 1:1 backed by ZEC in Coinbase custody. There is exactly one door for newly-minted cbZEC, and it is Coinbase's.

      1
      Deposit ZEC to your Coinbase account. Coinbase ZEC balances are transparent addresses; a send from any Zcash balance lands there in the open.
      2
      Choose "Send ZEC on Base" and give your Base wallet address. cbZEC arrives there.
      3
      Connect that wallet here. In v1 cbZEC can be swapped via CoW or LP'd once its gauge is voted; as collateral it is v1.1 — no lending market exists yet.
      Before you start.
      • Jurisdiction. Wrap/unwrap is unavailable in 100+ jurisdictions — all of the EEA, Australia, Brazil, Canada, Japan, Singapore, and New York. Effectively the US ex-NY (UK unverified). Non-US users can still buy cbZEC on Base; cbBTC/WETH users are unaffected.
      • KYC and custody. Full identity verification both ways. Coinbase knows the person, the amount and the destination address. Custodial, not private.
      • Transparent-only exit. Unwrapping pays ZEC to transparent addresses only. Fees, minimums and confirmation counts are unpublished.
      • Issuer powers. cbZEC is a B20 token: blocklist, pause, seize (burn blocked balances) and a rebase multiplier. cbBTC has none of these.

      Getting started

      You need one thing: a wallet on Base — Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, WalletConnect, or any EIP-6963 wallet. It signs; your Oilskin account owns.

      Your first position, in three steps

      1 · Connect, pick collateral (cbBTC or WETH; cbZEC as collateral is v1.1) and a strategy in New position.

      2 · Review the projection rail: net APY (sign shown honestly), health factor, liquidation price, fees, the forecast. Tick the acknowledgment. Nothing moves until you confirm.

      3 · Sign. Your first deposit is two transactions (account creation, then the router call); after that, one. Each step turns green only when its Base transaction confirms.

      There is no email and no account with us. There is KYC only if you enter through Coinbase to get cbZEC — see Risks & cbZEC.

      Ownership & the keeper

      OilskinAccount — an EIP-1167 clone per user; owner is your wallet, immutable after init. exec / execBatch are owner-only. It receives ERC-721/1155, has no admin, no upgrade, no fee logic, never caches a token balance across an external call (cbZEC rebases).

      OilskinAccountFactory — CREATE2; accountOf(owner) is known before the account exists; anyone may deploy it for that owner only.

      StrategyRouter — stateless. One transaction: Permit2 pull → venue.supply → venue.borrow(USDC) → swap if needed → lpVenue.open, every hop with a deadline and a bounded swap floor. It snapshots its balance of every token it will touch and asserts each is unchanged at exit — a delta, not an absolute zero, so a stray token anyone sends it is inert instead of bricking every open and unwind for everybody. Unwind is the mirror.

            Pools & the forecast

            Two models, and the band between them

            The published slice comes from a closed form that assumes the position never leaves its band. A real concentrated position spends part of the year outside it earning nothing, and pays a swap cost at every re-centre. So the gate prices every cell a second time with a Monte-Carlo-calibrated form — mcLpNet = netEmissions × inRangeEmissionsFactor + mcDragPct, exactly affine in the emissions rate, so two coefficients price the cell at every level including the boundary — and shows both numbers with the gap between them. Where they disagree the cell is inside the model's own uncertainty — the card says so, and the choice is yours.

            Rewards & claiming

            Every position sits staked in its pool's Aerodrome gauge. While staked, trading fees go to veAERO voters — the position earns AERO emissions instead. Claimable is the AERO accrued, net of the engine's and Oilskin's fees. Destinations: repay debt on Aave (raises your HF; usually the smartest choice while borrowing) or your wallet.

            Claim economics

            Withdrawals

            From your own account, in one transaction. Destinations mirror claims: repay debt (default while you have debt — you see HF before and after) or your wallet. Closing an LP position does not repay debt by itself — the preview says "debt unchanged" and means it; route the proceeds to "repay" if you want the loan down.

            The swap floor on the way out

            The slippage band

            Before any close the router reads the pool's slot0() and sets a price band; the transaction reverts rather than accept a sandwiched price. A price band, not a value floor — a single-token remainder's value is price-independent and a value floor would pass while the swap is robbed.

            Partial withdrawals

            The engine has no partial exit: the position closes and the remainder re-mints (60s hold restarts). Refunds from the re-mint are folded back single-sided; anything below the decimals-aware dust floor stays as idle and is paid on exit.

            FAQ

            Does my collateral get sold?

            Leveraged LP, hold and supply-only: never — it sits on Aave and comes back to your wallet. Spot: yes, that mode explicitly swaps it.

            Where is my position if I switch browsers?

            On Base, under your account. Connect the same wallet anywhere and the dashboard reads it from chain (our indexer is a cache, never the authority). This demo stores state in your browser and validates it on every load.

            Can Oilskin freeze or redirect a position I already have?

            No. Your account has no admin and no upgrade, the router asserts it ends every call holding exactly what it started with, and the keeper's permission is one function, bounded, expiring and revocable — you can always exec from your own account. What Oilskin can still do is choose which lending contract each asset uses for new positions, disable an asset immediately, and move the entry health floor. Changing a venue takes a fixed on-chain delay and is announced first. The full list is under Ownership & the keeper — we would rather write it down than claim it does not exist.
