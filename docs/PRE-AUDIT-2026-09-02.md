## The three questions this round answered

1. **"Where would engine refunds go?"** The engine swaps-to-ratio itself and
   refunds leftovers in the same transaction (typically <1%; a live receipt
   showed 0.88% of one leg). Before: stranded in a shared adapter. Now: booked
   idle to the position, redeployed at the next compound, paid out on exit.
2. **"No login — is that an issue?"** The locked payout address is the
   identity: lookup is read-only-safe by construction (funds can only exit to
   that address). The UI persists a local copy, offers find-by-address, and
   the production design binds a passkey at deposit (dust-proof fallback) for
   state-changing actions. See docs/V1-SIMPLE.md §identity.
3. **"Only include pools with true positive yield after fees and IL."**
   Measured and modeled: none qualify at 2026-08-31 rates — see
   docs/YIELD-REALITY-2026-08-31.md. The menu gate is now computed, not
   curated.
