### A.2 Notes on Rhea (the venue Oilskin uses today)

* **Liquidation threshold: confirmed 0.70 collateral factor, but the effective USDC-debt threshold is 0.665.** Burrow's health factor is `Σ(collateral × price × vol_ratio) / Σ(debt × price / vol_ratio_debt)`. ZEC `volatility_ratio` = 7000 and native USDC `volatility_ratio` = 9500, so an account is liquidatable when `USDC debt > 0.70 × 0.95 × ZEC value = 66.5%`. The repo's 0.70 assumption overstates the buffer by ~3.5 points. (https://github.com/NearDeFi/burrowland; https://api.burrow.finance/get_assets_paged_detailed, 2026-09-04)
* **Borrow rate is not 13.47% today; it is 15.37% on native USDC** (`17208628…33a1`, the asset NEAR Intents delivers). Utilization is 86.4% vs an 80% target, so the rate sits on the steep leg of the curve; `target_utilization_rate` and `max_utilization_rate` are per-ms compounding constants (`r^31,536,000,000 − 1`), so the rate will move sharply with utilization. Supply APR on that USDC is 9.96%, meaning USDC lenders on Rhea are the main beneficiaries of Oilskin's borrowing. (same API snapshot)
* Native USDC `net_tvl_multiplier` = 10000, ZEC = 0 — ZEC deposits earn no protocol-incentive weight.
* **Security history:** Rhea's margin-trading module (inside the Burrowland lending engine) was exploited for $18.4M on 2026-04-16; ~$4M in ZEC was routed into Zcash's shielded pool and is unrecoverable; USDC/ZEC markets were both touched; partial recoveries via Tether freeze, NEAR Intents intervention and attacker returns; compensation framework "being finalized". (https://rekt.news/rhea-finance-rekt, 2026-09-04)
* ZEC borrow cap is 1,500 ZEC live vs 5,000 in docs; ZEC supply cap 20,000. At ~$1,027/ZEC the whole ZEC market is ~$4.4M — a single mid-size Oilskin user would move the market.

### A.3 What it would take to borrow against ZEC on Base

* **Aave v3:** needs a Chainlink ZEC/USD feed on Base (none exists in Chainlink's registry as of 2026-09-04) plus an ARFC/risk-steward listing; cbZEC supply is 604 tokens, which is far below any listing threshold.
* **Morpho / Euler v2 (permissionless):** a market could be deployed tomorrow using a Pyth ZEC/USD adapter (feed exists) or a Uniswap TWAP, but it would need a curator willing to allocate USDC against it and the only on-chain liquidity for liquidators is ~$0.68M in one Aerodrome CL pool. Liquidation of even a $200K position would move the pool.
* **Compound v3:** governance listing + Chainlink feed; not plausible near-term.
* **Moonwell:** borrowing is frozen after the 2026-08-27 oracle exploit; would in any case be the wrong venue for a thin-liquidity collateral.

### B.2 Alternative designs — what the numbers imply (no recommendation)

Let L = loan-to-value drawn on ZEC (Rhea allows up to 66.5%; 40–50% is the usual practical band), r_b = borrow APR, Y = gross yield on borrowed USDC, and Oilskin's stack returns 76.5% of Y to the user (15% engine + 10% Oilskin).

1. **Borrow-and-farm (current design).** Net return on ZEC-denominated equity ≈ L × (0.765·Y − r_b). With r_b = 15.37% (Rhea native USDC, 2026-09-04) the break-even gross Y is **20.1%**. The best *low-IL* stable yields found are 4.5–6.5% (Morpho Gauntlet Prime 4.66%, Fluid 5.0%, PT-sUSDe 4.87%, Gauntlet Frontier ~6.5%); at Y = 6.5% the user loses ≈ L × 10.4% per year before any liquidation cost. Even at the Base-native borrow rate of 4.60% (Aave), break-even Y is 6.0% before fees — and no venue on Base accepts ZEC collateral today. Removing the 15% engine fee (direct positions) moves break-even at 15.37% borrow from 20.1% to 17.1% gross.
2. **Sell to stables and farm stables.** No IL, no liquidation, no borrow cost; 3–6.5% on blue-chip venues, 4.8–5.1% fixed via Pendle PTs to Nov-2026. Opportunity cost: ZEC moved +100.6% in the last 30 days and +2,455% in a year (CoinGecko, 2026-09-04). Whether a 5% stable yield is "worth it" is a question about the holder's view on ZEC, not about DeFi — the arithmetic says 5% is ~1.5 days of the recent ZEC move.
3. **Delta-neutral (spot ZEC + short ZEC perp, "Ethena on ZEC").** Funding is currently positive on most venues (~+0.01%/8h ≈ 11%/yr to shorts; Hyperliquid 7d avg ≈ 13%). This is the only observed ZEC-native yield above the Rhea borrow rate. Practical obstacles: (a) the short leg needs USD margin — borrowed at 15.37% or from selling ZEC; (b) margin liquidation on the short leg during +17% days (ZEC +8.2%/24h on read date); (c) funding flips (two venues negative on read date); (d) centralized-exchange counterparty risk or, on Hyperliquid, KYC-free but with its own risk; (e) the position is not "earning yield on ZEC without selling" in spirit — half the book is a synthetic short.
4. **Just lending ZEC.** DeFi pays essentially nothing (Rhea 0.076%). Maya LP pays 2.38% 30-day APR with CACAO exposure. Only CeFi pays "real" ZEC yield (OKX 5.72% flexible, WhiteBIT 5.15% locked) — custodial, KYC, unsecured. No permissionless venue pays a real ZEC supply rate, because there is almost no demand to borrow ZEC (362 ZEC borrowed protocol-wide on Rhea at 1.20%).

## C. Unknowns / could not verify

1. **Rhea front-end LTV cap.** The contract's only parameter is `volatility_ratio` (0.70). Whether the Rhea UI imposes a lower "max borrow" than the 66.5% liquidation line was not verifiable (client-rendered app).

## D. What the numbers say (no recommendation)

* **Best borrow rate found with ZEC as collateral, permissionless: 15.37% (Rhea, native USDC, 2026-09-04)** — higher, not lower, than the 13.47% the audit used. Legacy bridged stables on Rhea are cheaper (8.3–9.8%) if they can be delivered and if the extra bridge hop is acceptable. There is **no** permissionless venue on Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum or Solana where ZEC can currently be posted for a ~4–5% USDC loan; cbZEC is three days old, 604 tokens exist, no oracle-backed market lists it, and no Chainlink feed exists.
* **Effective Rhea liquidation line for USDC debt is 66.5% of ZEC value, not 70%.**
* **Best low-IL stable yield found: ~4.5–5.1% at scale** (Morpho Gauntlet USDC Prime 4.66% on $428M; Fluid Base 5.00% on $8.8M; PT-sUSDe fixed 4.87% to 2026-11-26; PT-srUSDe 5.14%). Anything above ~6.5% is small-TVL, exotic-collateral, or incentive-driven (Euler AlphaGrowth 18.9% on $0.46M; Pendle strUSD 11.8%; Aerodrome EURC/USDC 7.9% on $68K).
* **No stable-yield venue clears the Rhea borrow rate**: spread is −9 to −11 points before fees and before the 23.5% Oilskin/engine take; break-even gross yield at 15.37% borrow and 76.5% pass-through is 20.1%.
* The only ZEC-linked return above 15% observed today is **perp funding (~11–13% annualized to shorts)**, which requires taking the short side with USD margin and carries liquidation and sign-flip risk.
* **No permissionless venue pays meaningful ZEC supply yield** (Rhea 0.076%); CeFi pays up to 5.72% (OKX) with custody and KYC.
* Venue-risk backdrop in the last five months: Rhea margin exploit $18.4M (Apr-16), THORChain $10.7M exploit and 5-week halt (May-15), Zcash Orchard soundness bug and emergency upgrade (May-29 → Jul-28 Ironwood), Moonwell $8.7M oracle exploit (Aug-27), Seamless/Resolv bad debt. Any design that stacks three of these venues stacks their tail risks.
