## NI-HIGH-1 — a fee worth less than one USDC unit reverted the whole Close, and the keeper's protection with it

**Severity.** High. Protection that cannot fire on a reachable state: the keeper's `unwind` and
the web's single Close are the same router call, and both reverted. The raw exit through `exec`
was never affected (`invariant_userCanAlwaysExitViaExec` held throughout), which is why this is
not Critical — the user could always leave by hand; the product path, and the automated
protection the product tells the user is watching their loan, could not.

**Where.** `contracts/src/router/StrategyRouter.sol`, `_toUsdc` (the swap step of `unwind`, called
once per pool token on line 398 with each leg the LP close paid out). Before the fix it swapped
every non-zero, non-USDC leg unconditionally with the caller's quote.

**The failing scenario, executed.** The nightly configuration's first local run (109 s) reported
`invariant_singleCloseClearsEveryBook`: "a two-book single Close reverted although the account was
funded to cover every book", shrunk from 68 calls to 7: `switchVenue(true)` →
`supplyAndBorrowOnCurrentVenue(1.47e8, …)` (a Morpho book) → `openLp(200000)` →
`accrueYield(…, w = 6880, …)` (6,880 wei of WETH fee accrued on the position) → `switchVenue(false)`
→ `supplyAndBorrowOnCurrentVenue(9779, 4225)` (a second, tiny Aave book) → `singleCloseProbe()`.
Replayed deterministically with full traces (the scratch test is not committed; the regression
below carries the essence): the venue's close pays the account 6,192 wei of WETH (6,880 net of the
10 % performance fee) and 31,757 USDC; the router calls
`AerodromeSwapAdapter.swap(WETH, USDC, 6192, quotedIn 1e18, quotedOut 2,453.45e6, 100 bps)`; the
adapter's floor `6192 × 2453450000 × 9900 / (1e18 × 10000)` is **0**; `swap` refuses
`ZeroQuote()`; the account's `execWithCallback` bubbles it; the Close is gone. The two books are
incidental — the same happens on a one-book account; the deep run reached the state because
`accrueYield`'s WETH bound starts at 0 and only 180,000 calls per invariant sampled a value that
small next to an open position and a funded probe.

**Why it is real on Base and not only in the mock.** The engine's close pays both pool tokens: the
principal comes back one-sided (Addendum 5: 999.999999 USDC and 0 WETH), but fees accrue in both
tokens whenever the price crosses the range, and the AERO / WETH / cbZEC legs the engine and the
direct venue hand back are whatever the pool did — tiny amounts are the ordinary case, not the
edge. Any leg below `quotedIn / (quotedOut × (1 − tolerance))` — about 4.1 × 10⁸ wei of WETH at
2,453 USDC/WETH, i.e. anything under about one USDC unit of value — reverted the unwind. The
keeper builds its quote the same way (`agent/src/dispatch/quote.ts`), so a position that had earned
a few wei of the other token became a position the ladder could not repay.

**Why the fix is the adapter's floor and not a router constant.** The adapter's `minOutFor` is
the number the chain enforces (`RISKS.md` §13); a leg whose floor is zero could not be protected by
any swap the router is allowed to send — the adapter would refuse it by name. So "dust" is defined
by the code that would have refused it, at the caller's own quote and tolerance, never by a typed
threshold. Below the floor the token stays in the account (the user's, sweepable, closable later);
at or above it the leg is swapped exactly as before. An empty quote is still refused: the caller
gave nothing to protect the swap with, and that is the adapter's decision, not this one.

**What was not changed.** The keeper does not yet read `DustLegKept` (its receipt summary is about
`VenueRepaid` / `VenueWithdrawn`; a kept dust leg changes neither), and the web's Close plan does
not yet say a dust leg may be left behind — both are copy, listed here as open, not silently
assumed. The direct venue's path shares `_toUsdc`, so the cbZEC / AERO legs get the same rule with
no separate change.

**ABI.** One new event on `StrategyRouter`: `DustLegKept(address indexed account,address indexed
token,uint256 amount)`; `contracts/abi/oilskin-abi.json` and `web/lib/abi/oilskin.generated.ts`
regenerated, the keeper's seam unchanged (`docs/CONTRACT-ABI.md`).

**Regression.** `contracts/test/audit-regressions/DustLegClose.t.sol`; the invariant that found it
is unchanged and now green at the nightly depth. `docs/TESTING.md` carries the counts.
