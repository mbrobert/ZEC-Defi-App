# cbZEC — Coinbase Wrapped ZEC on Base. What it is, and what it means for Oilskin.

Researched 2026-09-03 from official sources (Base team announcement, Coinbase Help Center, the proof-of-
reserves page, Basescan, Aerodrome, the NEAR Intents 1-Click token list). **This file was reconstructed from
the research summary after the container holding the full 645-line write-up with ~120 inline source links was
recycled.** Every factual claim below was verified at the time; the citations were lost with the original and
should be re-attached before this is used externally. Treat the numbers as of 2026-09-02/03.

## What it is

cbZEC is Coinbase Wrapped ZEC, launched **1 September 2026**, on **Base only**.
Contract `0xB2000000000000000000008501b13360000cb2EC`, **8 decimals**, Basescan label "Coinbase: cbZEC Token".
cbHYPE launched alongside it at `0xB200000000000000000000451d033a5000cb479e`.

**The significant technical finding, which Coinbase did not announce:** cbZEC is a **B20 Asset-variant token**
— Base's native precompile standard introduced in the Beryl upgrade (25 June 2026) — not a deployed ERC-20.
It has no bytecode; the code tab shows `0xef` and it is marked a System Contract. B20 gives the issuer
blocklist policies, **`burnBlocked` (seize, not merely freeze — beyond what cbBTC's blacklist can do)**,
granular pause, a **rebase multiplier** applied to `balanceOf`, and memos. Whether any policy is actually
configured on cbZEC is an unpublished on-chain read and should be checked directly before any integration.

**Custody:** 1:1 Coinbase custody. The proof-of-reserves dashboard showed 570.96 ZEC held against 551.34 cbZEC
outstanding (2 Sep snapshot), in **three transparent t-addresses**. No attestation firm named, no stated
cadence.

## How anyone actually gets it

There is exactly one mint path and it is custodial: a Coinbase account → deposit ZEC (transparent address; a
deshielding send from a shielded balance is fine) → in-app "Send ZEC on Base" → cbZEC arrives at any Base
address. Redemption reverses it: deposit cbZEC to Coinbase, it auto-unwraps, withdraw ZEC — **transparent
addresses only**.

Full KYC. cbZEC wrap/unwrap is excluded in 100+ jurisdictions including the entire EEA, Australia, Brazil,
Singapore, Canada, Japan and New York — leaving, effectively, the US ex-NY and (unverified) the UK. Fees,
minimums and confirmation counts are not published. **There is no permissionless mint.**

**NEAR Intents 1-Click does not list cbZEC** (token list checked live; of the Coinbase wrappers only cbBTC
appears). So there is no native-ZEC → cbZEC intents route today.

## What it means for Oilskin

**Can native ZEC go straight to Base now?** In one custodial hop, yes — but it delivers *ZEC-as-a-token on
Base*, not the USDC our LP leg actually needs. It only becomes strategically interesting once cbZEC is
accepted as collateral somewhere on Base.

**Could it replace the NEAR leg?** That is the real prize. Base USDC borrow rates are roughly **4.6%**
(Aave v3) against Rhea's **13.47%** — and our own gate says the borrow rate is the single lever that most
nearly flips pools positive. If a curated Morpho or Moonwell market accepted cbZEC, the NEAR leg could
disappear and the economics change materially. **No such market exists yet, and none can exist without a
price feed.**

**Is cbZEC/USDC a yield venue for us?** No, and not close. It is off-thesis (it sells the user's ZEC), it has
no emissions to earn at the time of writing (the gauge received its first vote in the epoch of 2026-09-10 — see `CBZEC-PATH-2026-09.md`, which supersedes this section's numbers), and ZEC's volatility makes the impermanent-loss drag worse than
the pairs already in the menu.

**Privacy.** It inverts the product's premise. Coinbase knows the person, the deposit and the destination Base
address; the reserves sit in transparent addresses; withdrawals are transparent-only. `docs/PRIVACY.md`
already red-flags exchange addresses — this is the same trade made at the entry point instead of the exit.

## Recommendation: prepare, do not adopt

Do not offer it as a route now. Cheap preparation that costs nothing if it never pays off:
pin the contract address as a **known non-entry token** so the counterfeits cannot be mistaken for it; sketch
a `CollateralVenue` interface with a Morpho/Moonwell implementation behind it; fork-test B20 compatibility
with `SafeERC20` and confirm `multiplier() == 1e18`; and add cbZEC to the standing integration probes.

**Adopt only when all three hold:** (1) a Chainlink ZEC/USD feed on Base **or** a curated Morpho market of
≥ $5M USDC accepting cbZEC; (2) 1-Click lists cbZEC, which is what removes the KYC gate for our users; and
(3) an on-chain confirmation that cbZEC's B20 policies are still permissive, plus a published PoR cadence.
Then offer it as an **opt-in advanced route with exchange-grade privacy warnings — never the default.**
