# Internal audit — every module re-read after seven weeks, and what the chain changed meanwhile (2026-11-16)

Four read-only adversarial passes on the tree at `2fa1b0e`'s parent line (`main`, 2026-11-16): the
Base contracts, the keeper and shared library, the Solana program with the yield service and the
web's non-visual logic, and a re-read of the outside world the product depends on. Each fix below
was made only after a failing test reproduced the finding; the fix commit is the one that adds this
file. Nothing is deployed, so no finding here touched anyone's funds.

Abbreviations: HF = health factor; LP = liquidity provision; LTV = loan-to-value; CCTP = Circle's
Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol; IOC = immediate-or-cancel; RPC = remote procedure call.

## The outside world

### EXT-1 · High (product) · Aerodrome became Aero; the pools the product deposits into earn nothing

`docs/VERIFIED-BASE-FACTS.md` Addendum 18 has the reads. Aerodrome's emissions ended at the epoch
flip of 2026-10-22 00:00 UTC; every old gauge reads `isAlive = false`. Aero is a separate deployment
with a different gauge interface (`rewardRate()` and `periodFinish()` revert; claiming is
`claimEmissions`). The MaxFi/Snuggle engine has added sixteen Uniswap v3 entries since September
and none on Aero's factory; its three curated Aerodrome entries still point at the old pools.

- **Scenario.** A user opens `aero-cbbtc-usdc` through the engine. The position is staked in a dead
  gauge: no emissions, and a staked Slipstream position collects no swap fees. They pay the borrow
  rate for nothing.
- **What already holds.** The yield service zeroes emissions when `periodFinish <= now`
  (`services/yield/src/sources/gauges.ts:272`, `forecast.ts:344`, `server.ts:368`), so a live
  forecast shows these pools as negative and the user must acknowledge that before depositing.
  The demo snapshot of 2026-09-13 predates the change and still shows September's emissions,
  dated as a snapshot.
- **What does not.** `SlipstreamLpVenue`, `AerodromeSwapAdapter`, `Deploy.s.sol`'s router address,
  the yield service's emissions read, and `packages/shared/src/pools.ts` are all written against
  the old deployment.
- **Decision needed (founder).** Either integrate Aero directly (new venue against CLGauge, new
  swap adapter, a new emissions read — a design note first), or wait for the engine to list Aero
  pools, or launch the Base LP leg on the engine's Uniswap v3 pools (fees only, no emissions to
  price). Until one is chosen the Aerodrome pools should not be presented as an earning option.

### EXT-2 · Info · cbZEC has a lending market on Base

Morpho Blue market `0xe458a501…1327`: USDC against cbZEC, LLTV 62.5 %, 3.94 M USDC supplied
(Addendum 18 §4). Decision D3's condition — "until a Base lending market lists it" — is met. The
product's statement that no such market exists was false and is corrected in
`packages/shared/src/zecForms.ts`, `zecRoutes.ts`, `web/lib/copy.ts`, `web/app/layout.tsx`,
`README.md` and both prototypes. cbZEC stays disabled: the market's `BASE_FEED_1` has not been
identified, and switching the asset on is the founder's registry decision.

Also read, not acted on: Circle pauses CCTP **V1** contracts on 2026-12-01 (this code is V2 only);
klend has shipped releases 1.26 → 1.28 since the Solana facts were read, so the decoders in
`agent/src/solana/layouts.ts` and the program's CPI layouts need a re-read against the live program
before any deploy; Next 16 / React 19 / Tailwind 4 / wagmi 3 are current and the web is one major
behind on each — an upgrade, not a defect.

## Keeper

### K-H1 · High · A crossing that returned FAILED once was re-dispatched as a new rung

`agent/src/solana/monitor.ts` (resume, ~240-257 before the fix). Resume sent only `SENT` records to
the stage machine; anything else went to `dispatch()`, which never looked at `record.bridge`.

- **Scenario.** A rung-3 `closeLpAndBurn` lands on Base. The next tick's receipt read gets an RPC
  5xx → FAILED. The tick after, `dispatch()` sees health still low (nothing has been delivered),
  closes more of the owner's LP and burns again; the new result overwrites the first burn's nonce
  and message, so the first burn is never delivered.
- **Fix.** A record whose bridge is not yet `delivered` always returns to `confirm`, counts as in
  flight, is not superseded by a more severe rung or by a recovered episode (K-M3), is never
  overwritten by a different burn (that escalates, naming both hashes), and does not spend its
  attempt budget while waiting on Circle. Transient failures escalate once at the attempt cap and
  keep retrying — what is left is idempotent and spends none of the user's money; permanent
  failures still end ABANDONED with an escalation.
- **Regression.** `agent/test/solana-monitor.test.ts`, "a crossing in flight is never forgotten
  (AUDIT 2026-11-16 H-1, M-3, L-2)" — seven tests.
- **Corrects** `AUDIT-2026-09-25.md` CC-2 and O-8, which said the persisted nonce and the next
  tick's re-read settled this. They did not.

### K-M1 · Medium · `planProtect` asked for one unit more than existed

`agent/src/solana/policy.ts`. `Math.ceil((x / 1e6) * 1e6) === x + 1` for about 1.2 % of amounts
(128000008 → 128000009 in the regression). The program's `account_usdc.amount >= repay_usdc` then
failed on every tick with the same plan. Fixed by clamping in bigint. Regression: two tests named
"M-1 (2026-11-16)" in `agent/test/solana-policy.test.ts`.

### K-M2 · Medium · A send that never reached SENT could be sent again beside itself

`agent/src/dispatch/keeperDispatcher.ts`. `writeContract` was never given the nonce the dispatcher
had just persisted. A deadline error or an aborted tick left the record PENDING; resume re-read a
world the unmined first transaction had not changed and sent a second full-size call at the next
nonce. Fixed: every send is pinned to the persisted nonce; a `replayGuard` waits while something
under that nonce is unmined, proceeds when it has been consumed (the world read then sizes the
replay) and refuses when the nonce cannot be read. Waiting was chosen over same-nonce replacement
because one keeper address serves many accounts. Regression:
`agent/test/fixM2-nonce-replay.test.ts` (seven tests).
**Open:** `agent/src/perps/dispatcher.ts` ~209-227 has the same shape and no guard yet.

### K-M3, K-L2 — fixed with K-H1. K-L1, K-L3 — open, as in the table.

K-L1's mismatches are at entry HF 1.2750 and 1.6050 (all four disarm levels, 0.01 apart) and at
2.0450, 2.1550, 2.4850, 2.8150 (warn only). Each chain is self-consistent; only a surface that
previews a Solana position with the float `ladderFor` is off. The seam test steps by 0.01 and
should step by 1 bp.

## Contracts

Nothing above Low was found in the router, the account and its grant budgets, the Aave and Morpho
venues, both LP venues, the swap adapters or the CCTP burns. Both Mediums are in the perps venue.

### PV-1 · Medium · A full `reduce` deleted the entry record on intent

`contracts/src/venues/HyperliquidPerpVenue.sol`, `reduce`. The reduce-only IOC order is only
*sent* in that transaction; HyperCore fills it — or not — seconds later. With the record gone,
every keeper `protect` reverted `NoEntry`, which the keeper treats as permanent: an open short
with no protection and nothing on chain saying so. Fixed: a full reduce leaves the record; a flat
account is recognised from the live read (`NoPosition`), and the next `open` overwrites the record.
`EntryCleared` stays in the interface and is no longer emitted. Regression:
`contracts/test/audit-regressions/PerpEntryLifetime.t.sol` (three tests).

### PV-2 · Medium · An owner improving a stressed short was refused

Same file, `reduce` and `addMargin` → `_reserveE6` → `PerpHealthLib.ladderFor`, which reverts under
an equivalent HF of 1.10. At 10× (maintenance margin 500 bps), account value 1,000 on notional
10,000 is HF 1.0499; cutting a quarter gives 1.0861 and reverted. Fixed: below the minimum the
action goes through and the record keeps the previous entry's ladder with the new size — recording
1.10 instead would put the rungs under the live HF and stop the keeper acting. Regression:
`contracts/test/audit-regressions/PerpStressedOwnerAction.t.sol` (five tests).

C-L1, C-L2, C-L3 are open, as in the table.

## Solana program

### S-PA · High · FIXED · A collateral sale was not tied to a payment by the keeper

`solana/programs/oilskin/src/instructions/keeper_protect.rs:131-173`. The design
(`SOLANA-ARCHITECTURE.md` §3) says collateral leaves only "under a delegation sized by a payment
already received". The instruction checks only `account_usdc.amount >= repay_usdc`.

- **Scenario.** The account holds idle USDC — the wizard's default "keep" choice leaves the whole
  borrow there. HF falls under the repay rung. A keeper key calls
  `keeper_protect(repay_usdc = X, sell_zec = Y)` and transfers nothing in. The program repays X of
  the user's own USDC, withdraws Y ZEC and approves the keeper for it. HF rises, so
  `ProtectionIneffective` passes. The keeper pulls Y ZEC having paid nothing.
- **Bound.** Idle USDC and the grant's budgets; who can do it is a compromised or misbehaving
  keeper key inside a live grant. The honest keeper goes repay-only when idle USDC covers the need.
- **Proposed fix.** When `sell_zec > 0`, pull the payment inside the instruction: a `keeper_usdc`
  account whose authority is the keeper signer, transferred to `account_usdc` before the repay,
  with at least the sale floor required to arrive that way.
- **Fix (the commit after the one that added this file).** `keeper_protect` takes a fourth argument,
  `keeper_usdc_in`, and a `keeper_usdc` token account whose authority must be the keeper signer. The
  instruction transfers that amount keeper → Account itself, before the repay, and the sale floor is
  held against it instead of against `repay_usdc`. A payment with no sale, or one larger than the
  repay, is refused `KeeperPaymentMisused` (appended to the error list, so no existing code moved).
  The keeper's dispatcher no longer prepends a transfer; `agent/src/solana/layouts.ts` and the IDL
  seam (`agent/scripts/verify-solana-idl.mjs`) carry the new shape, and `solana/idl/oilskin.json` and
  the web's generated copy are re-synced.
- **Regression.** `solana/tests/ladder.spec.ts`, the de-risk test: with the owner's idle USDC enough
  to cover the whole repay, a sale the keeper pays nothing for and one it pays 1 % under the floor
  for are both refused `SaleBelowFloor`, a payment without a sale and one above the repay are
  refused `KeeperPaymentMisused`, none of them moves the owner's USDC, and the paid sale debits the
  keeper exactly what it named. Run against the unchanged program first, the unpaid sale returned OK.
  Localnet: **36 passing** on a validator started for this (`RPC_PORT=9100`, `FIXTURES=fixtures-e2`).
- **Two things the run turned up.** (1) `tests/keeper.spec.ts` allowed 10 base units between the
  debt's fall and the keeper's payment; interest accrued between the two reads is now 16 of 1.77e9
  at the borrow rate the validator clones today, so the tolerance is one part in ten million of the
  debt. The same assertion failed on the unchanged program. (2) The 4.2.2 test validator refuses
  SBPF v0–v2 programs ("sbpf_version required by the executable which are not enabled") and accepts
  v3, Anchor 1.2.0's default; a build made with `--arch v0` cannot be deployed to it, and a stale
  ledger directory can report the opposite feature set. Which SBPF version **mainnet** accepts on
  the deploy day was not established here and must be read before `SOLANA-DEPLOY.md` is run.

S-PB is open (inferred): `close_position` skips the repay when debt is 1–100 base units and then
withdraws everything; klend is expected to refuse. Workaround: `repay(u64::MAX)` first.

## Yield service and web

### W-A · Medium · A live session fell back to the demo snapshot and could still sign

`web/lib/solana/hooks.ts:47-54` and `web/lib/hooks.ts:52-62` return the recorded snapshot when the
live read fails, with `stale: false`. With the yield service down, a connected wallet's borrow was
sized at September's ZEC price; the health factor on chain could land far from the one shown.
Fixed: `web/lib/solana/sign-gate.ts` (`canSignWithView`, `canSignWithMarketSource`) — in a
connected session, the step before signing and the sign action itself require a live, fresh read,
and the page says why it is blocked. Demo mode is unchanged. Regression:
`web/test/solana-sign-gate.test.ts`.

### W-C · Low — fixed in `web/lib/solana/plan.ts`; regression in `web/test/solana-plan.test.ts`.

### Y-A · Medium · OPEN · The cross-chain forecast deploys the reserve too

`services/yield/src/forecast.ts:403-406`, `web/lib/forecast.ts:348-352`. The user's net is
`supply + LTV × (lpNet − borrow)` on the whole borrow, but in the loop the reserve fraction stays
idle on Solana. The forecast is high by `LTV × R × lpNet`. The fix is one shared function that
both the service and the web use: `supply + LTV × ((1 − R) × lpNet − borrow)`.

### W-B · Medium · OPEN (inferred) · Closing a quiet Solana position can fail every time

`web/lib/solana/execute.ts:132-134` tops up to the obligation's cached debt + 0.1 %; klend accrues
interest into that field only on refresh, and `close_position` refreshes first. A position
untouched for longer than 0.1 % of a year's interest reverts `InsufficientUsdcToClose`, and the
revert discards the refresh. To be confirmed on localnet before it is fixed.
