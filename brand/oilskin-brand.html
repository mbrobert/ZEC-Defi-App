The idea

The mark is the shed line. A waxed shoulder in linen, one bead of rain sitting proud, run-off rolling away. It's the product in one picture: the storm is real, the coat holds, and the person underneath keeps working. Never a shield, never a vault door — Oilskin protects by shedding, not by locking away.

Voice: plain, warm, worked-in. Say what's public, what's protected, and who holds what — before the user asks. Never "military-grade", never "100% safe", never an asterisk.

Type & texture

  • Wordmark: heavy geometric caps, tracked wide (letter-spacing .13em). App type: the system sans stack, weights 400/650/800. Numerals always tabular.
  • Buy later, if wanted: a slab or grotesk with workwear character for headlines (e.g. Archivo / Roboto Slab class). Never a techno/crypto display face.
  • Texture: linen grain and waxed-canvas photography welcome in marketing; the app itself stays flat linen with soft warm shadows.

Don'ts

  • No gold UI accents (gold belongs to the ZEC asset mark alone). No dark-purple DeFi gradients. No shields, locks, or vault doors.
  • Never put brick text on brick tint for emphasis-stacking; never use critical red decoratively.
  • The bead always sits on the line, never falling — the coat works.
